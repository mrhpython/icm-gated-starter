# The map

Before you touch anything, read `CONTEXT.md`. It says what we are building right now and where the
material is.

Stages live in `stages/`, numbered. One stage, one job. Each stage's `CONTEXT.md` lists its inputs,
its process, its audit, and its outputs. Read only the stage you are on.

Every draft goes through `stages/03-verify` before anything is published. You never grade your own
draft; the gate does. A gate result you cannot read is UNKNOWN, and UNKNOWN never ships.

Publishing is the person's. Stop at `stages/04-live` and hand back.

When a job ends, write what happened in `CONTEXT.md` under "Where we left off".
